## Key Findings

- **Root cause of failures:** Describer prompt asked only for mechanism (inputs/outputs/side-effects). Context — domain, purpose, problem prevented — was stripped.
- `terminal-buffering` MRR=0.00: "terminal" and "IPC" never appeared in the description.
- `concurrency-limiter` MRR=0.00: "heap exhaustion" and "chat sessions" in the file's own Why comment were ignored.
- R@3 (92%) vs R@1 (60%) gap suggests descriptions are capturing enough to find the file, but not enough to rank it first.
- All 5 negatives pass at sim < 0.5 — no false positives.
